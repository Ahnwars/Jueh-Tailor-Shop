1. Privacy Policy

Business Information

Jueh Tailoring ("we," "us," or "our") is a custom apparel and jersey shop located at Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon, Philippines. For privacy inquiries, contact us via Facebook Messenger at m.me/JuehTailoring.

We comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and the Internet Transactions Act of 2023 (RA 11967).

Information We Collect

When you place an order through our website, we collect:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Your name and phone number or email address.
  • Order Data: Item type, quantity, needed-by date, and any notes you provide.
  • Usage Data: Basic browser and device information collected automatically by our hosting platform (GitHub Pages).

We do not collect payment card details, body measurements, government IDs, or sensitive personal information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your custom tailoring order.
  • To communicate with you about order status, updates, or follow-up questions.
  • To generate and track your Order ID for order lookup.

Data Storage & Third Parties

Order data is stored in Google Sheets via Google Apps Script, hosted on Google's servers under Google's Privacy Policy. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages under GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

Data Retention

Order records are retained for a minimum of one (1) year for business and legal compliance purposes, after which they may be deleted at the owner's discretion.

Your Rights (RA 10173)

As a Philippine data subject, you have the right to be informed, access your data, correct inaccuracies, and request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, message us directly via Facebook Messenger.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without verifiable parental consent.

Nature of Service — Contract for a Piece of Work

All orders constitute a Contract for a Piece of Work under Article 1713 of the Civil Code of the Philippines. Custom production begins upon your order confirmation and our acceptance.

Order Accuracy & Customer Responsibility

You are responsible for providing accurate order details (item type, quantity, sizing specifications, needed-by date, and contact information). Jueh Tailoring shall not be liable for errors or delays caused by inaccurate information submitted through the order form.

Cancellations & Non-Refundable Deposits

Once production has commenced (fabric has been cut or sewing has begun), orders cannot be cancelled and any deposits paid are non-refundable, in accordance with RA 11967 and the custom, non-resellable nature of made-to-measure goods.

Cancellation requests before production begins may be accommodated at the owner's discretion. Contact us as soon as possible via Messenger.

Alterations & Fit

We will make reasonable adjustments if a finished garment deviates from agreed specifications. Alterations due to customer-submitted measurement errors or changes in order specifications after production begins may incur additional fees.

3. Cookie Policy

What We Use

This website uses minimal browser storage to improve your experience:

  • Session Storage: Used temporarily (within your browser tab) to remember your passcode on the owner/admin pages so you don't need to re-enter it on refresh. This data is never sent to our servers and is cleared when you close the tab.
  • URL Parameters: After submitting an order, your Order ID and contact info are passed as URL parameters to the order-lookup page for convenience. These are not stored beyond that page visit.

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or third-party marketing cookies.

GitHub Pages

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages (Microsoft), may collect standard server logs including your IP address and browser information for infrastructure and security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Managing Storage

You can clear session storage at any time by closing your browser tab or clearing your browser's site data. This will log you out of the owner/admin pages.
